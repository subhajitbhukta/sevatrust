Two forces make a unified digital platform urgent for the Trust today — fundraising growth and client connectivity.

FUNDRAISING GROWTH

Donors expect a digital, receiptable, transparent experience

Cash-and-cheque donations are declining across India while UPI and online giving grow 28% year-on-year. Donors now expect instant 80G receipts, campaign progress visibility, and the ability to give in under 60 seconds — or they give elsewhere.

28% YoY growth in Indian online donations

Beneficiaries, volunteers, partners expect a single front door

Today a beneficiary, a CSR partner, and a volunteer each face different, fragmented touchpoints. A single platform gives every stakeholder one consistent, traceable front door — closing the trust loop between intent, action, and impact.

3 stakeholder types, 1 platform
